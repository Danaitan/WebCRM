# Customer selection frame across contract expansion

The change gives the selected customer row a blue outer frame and extends that frame around the adjacent contract-detail row while expanded. Selection is initialized on the first result and shared between ordinary row clicks and the inline expand control; contract cards are populated asynchronously after the shared contract request completes. Two transitions allow the visible expanded detail to drift away from the selected row, and the inactive-selected background rule is masked by the cell-level selected styling. **Watch for:** **[Medium, confirmed]** selecting another row leaves the previous detail expanded outside the selected frame; **[Medium, confirmed]** opening a non-active row is undone by its async card update; **[Low, confirmed]** inactive selected rows keep the blue selected background rather than the declared gray background.

**Verdict**: NEEDS_CHANGES

## High-level view

The customer selection is represented by both `active-row` and `selected-contract-row`, while expansion is represented separately on the customer row and its adjacent card row. Ordinary row selection updates only the selection classes, so an already expanded card can remain attached to a now-unselected customer and lose the shared blue frame.

The inline control selects a non-active row, starts its asynchronous customer load, and immediately expands the card. When that load completes, `updateContractCard` unconditionally restores the card to collapsed state, making the expanded UI close itself after the user opens it.

The inactive-selected background override is placed on the `<tr>`, while the selected blue background is an `!important` declaration on each `<td>`, so the intended inactive background cannot appear.

## Row selection leaves the previous contract detail detached

**[Medium, confirmed]** The normal row-click path calls `setActiveCustomerRow` and loads the new customer, but it never clears `data-expanded`, hides the old card row, or removes `contract-expanded` from the previous customer (`webCRM/wwwroot/js/customerDetail.js:780-802`). `setActiveCustomerRow` only transfers `active-row` and `selected-contract-row` (`webCRM/wwwroot/js/customerDetail.js:656-675`). Because the expanded-frame CSS requires the visible card to immediately follow a `selected-contract-row`, the old detail remains visible but stops participating in the blue outer frame. This also violates the inline-toggle path's one-expanded-row policy and can reappear after company filtering because the stale card still has `data-expanded="true"`.

Collapse the previous expanded card whenever selection moves, or explicitly transfer expansion to the newly selected row while keeping `data-expanded`, `contract-expanded`, visibility, ARIA state, and chevron state synchronized.

## Async card population reverses an inline expansion

**[Medium, confirmed]** Clicking the inline toggle on a non-active row selects it and starts `loadCustomerSelection` without awaiting it, then opens the card and marks it expanded (`webCRM/wwwroot/js/customerDetail.js:2216-2261`). Completion of that selection load calls `updateContractCard`, which unconditionally sets `data-expanded="false"`, hides the card, removes `contract-expanded`, and resets the toggle (`webCRM/wwwroot/js/customerDetail.js:1300-1324`). The result is a user-visible open-then-close transition whose timing depends on the customer-detail request.

Preserve the card's pre-update expansion state during `updateContractCard`, or finish selection/card population before applying the user's requested expansion. The restored state must update the row class, detail-row visibility, `data-expanded`, `aria-expanded`, and chevron together.

## Inactive-selected background override loses the cascade

**[Low, confirmed]** `.active-row > td` applies `#eff6ff !important` directly to every selected cell (`webCRM/wwwroot/css/customerDetail.css:99-102`), while `.inactive-customer-row.active-row` applies the intended `#f8fafc !important` only to the row (`webCRM/wwwroot/css/customerDetail.css:492-494`). Cell backgrounds paint over the row background, so inactive selected customers remain blue despite the explicit inactive-selected rule.

Target the inactive selected row's cells with sufficient specificity, while retaining the same 2px blue border declarations if selection is still meant to be visible.
